# 90-Day Transcenlutions Acquisition Proof Plan

**Start:** October 7, 2026  
**Day 90:** January 4, 2027  
**Goal:** Prove the Acquisition Research & Support service on Transcenlutions first while pursuing a responsible low-cash acquisition/partnership into real cash flow.

## Operating rules

- Begin each active workday with prayer and end the work cycle with review/prayer.
- Revenue and verified cash flow outrank vanity activity.
- Friday work must finish before sunset; Saturday daytime is Sabbath rest — no business execution.
- Research and drafting may be automated; consequential actions remain governed.
- Never fabricate financials, business age, credit history, revenue, contracts or lender eligibility.
- Do not buy/reinstate an entity, submit credit, sign NDA/LOI/purchase documents, pay fees, move money, or make legal/financing commitments without required approval and professional review.
- A disciplined PASS on a bad acquisition is a successful result.

## Success metrics

By Day 90 we want evidence of:

- at least two real acquisition candidates fully analyzed or a documented market-based reason none qualified;
- verified financing pathways and personal-guarantee/down-payment realities;
- a professional handoff network/process appropriate to the actual scope;
- one repeatable evidence-backed acquisition research package;
- a measured cost-to-deliver and pricing hypothesis;
- a safe, governed limited-pilot decision;
- actual revenue recorded separately from projected revenue;
- no deceptive aged-entity/credit claims, unauthorized legal work, or unapproved financing commitments.
