## Purpose

Transcenlutions Acquisition Research & Support helps a buyer identify, verify, compare, finance, and coordinate the acquisition of a real operating business while keeping regulated and legally consequential work with appropriately licensed professionals.

The internal proof case is Transcenlutions LLC itself. We will use the system to search for and evaluate service businesses with real cash flow, recurring/repeat customers, low owner dependence, and financing structures that minimize upfront owner cash without fabricating creditworthiness or hiding liabilities.

## Core customer outcome

A client should receive an evidence-backed acquisition command package that answers:

1. What businesses appear to fit the buyer's goals?
2. What is verified versus only claimed by a seller/broker?
3. What would the business look like after realistic management cost, debt service, reserves, and working capital?
4. What financing structures appear available?
5. What documents and professional reviews are still required?
6. What is the next owner decision?

## Service scope Transcenlutions may perform

- Define buyer criteria and acquisition buy box.
- Research public and authorized listing sources.
- Normalize and deduplicate acquisition candidates.
- Separate seller/broker claims from source-supported facts.
- Build evidence-backed candidate summaries and comparison tables.
- Analyze owner dependence, recurring revenue, customer concentration, staffing, transferability, and operating systems.
- Build non-binding cash-flow and financing scenarios from client-supplied or source-supported numbers.
- Build due-diligence request lists and missing-document trackers.
- Coordinate timelines, approvals, questions, meetings, and handoffs.
- Research SBA, SSBCI, seller financing, conventional lending, CDFI, investor, grant, and strategic-partner pathways.
- Prepare non-binding questions and communications for owner approval.
- Maintain acquisition deal rooms, evidence provenance, audit history, and owner-attention queues inside Tay.
- Prepare post-acquisition Day 0 / Day 1 / 30 / 90 operating integration plans.

## Explicitly excluded / licensed-professional handoff

Transcenlutions must not present itself as a law firm, CPA firm, licensed lender, securities broker-dealer, investment adviser, real-estate brokerage, formal business appraiser, or bankruptcy professional unless a properly licensed professional/entity is actually engaged in that role.

Route the following to qualified professionals as applicable:

### Attorney
- purchase agreement, LOI legal terms, representations/warranties, indemnities
- entity/asset structure legal advice
- contract assignment/novation and employment/legal compliance
- liens, litigation, bankruptcy, successor-liability and closing-law issues
- real-estate closing/title work where required

### CPA / qualified accounting professional
- quality of earnings
- tax-return reconciliation
- tax exposure and transaction-tax structure
- normalization of owner compensation/add-backs where professional assurance is needed
- working-capital and accounting diligence

### Licensed lender / financing professional
- actual underwriting
- loan application and credit decision
- regulated lending activity
- final loan structure and disclosures

### Licensed securities / M&A professional where applicable
- securities transactions outside an applicable exemption
- capital raising, investor solicitation, securities placement or transaction-based compensation where registration may be required

### Licensed real-estate professional where applicable
- activities requiring a real-estate license when a transaction includes regulated real-property brokerage activity

### Bankruptcy counsel
- any bankruptcy strategy, filing, restructuring or creditor-rights analysis

## Regulatory guardrails

- No legal advice or preparation of legal instruments by nonlawyers.
- No promise that an acquisition, grant, loan, seller-finance structure or no-PG structure will be approved.
- No use of dormant/shelf entities as a misrepresentation of operating history, revenue, creditworthiness or repayment capacity.
- No loan or credit application, hard pull, entity reinstatement, seller contact, NDA, LOI, purchase agreement, financing commitment, payment or filing without the owner/client's required approval.
- Do not take transaction-based securities compensation unless counsel confirms the activity is permitted and any required licensing/exemption conditions are satisfied.
- Do not perform regulated real-estate brokerage activity unless appropriately licensed.
- All material facts preserve source, date, claim/evidence status and material unknowns.

## Internal proof target

Before public launch, Transcenlutions must complete at least one documented internal acquisition search through these gates:

`BUY BOX -> DISCOVERY -> VERIFICATION -> SHORTLIST -> FINANCING SCENARIOS -> PROFESSIONAL HANDOFF MAP -> DUE-DILIGENCE PLAN -> OWNER DECISION`

The proof does not require Transcenlutions to close an acquisition if no responsible deal exists. A disciplined PASS can be a valid successful proof when evidence shows the deal is not acceptable.

## Initial Transcenlutions buy box

Prioritize:

- South Carolina first, especially Columbia/Midlands and reachable service territories
- commercial cleaning, property maintenance, exterior cleaning, grounds/lawn, facilities support, and adjacent recurring B2B service businesses
- existing recurring/repeat revenue
- transferable commercial relationships/contracts
- enough staff/contractor capacity that the owner does not have to perform the core labor
- seller transition support
- seller financing or low-cash structures where legitimate
- documented books that can be reconciled
- positive cash flow after realistic manager replacement cost and debt service

Hard-reject or heavily penalize:

- unverifiable books
- hidden/unclear debt, liens, tax issues or litigation
- owner-dependent labor with no replacement economics
- non-transferable customers or licenses
- unsupported 'no personal guarantee' claims
- aged-shell/reinstatement strategies whose primary value is only entity age/EIN
- cash flow that fails after realistic payroll, management, working capital and debt service
