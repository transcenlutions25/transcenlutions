# Transcenlutions Founder Canon Directive

Status: CANON
Founder decision date: 2026-09-07
Authority: Direct founder instruction

## Directive

All Transcenlutions/Tay systems, features, characters, workflows, policies, logics, designs, inspirations, and architectural concepts that have been established, approved, requested, or affirmatively adopted by the founder in accessible Transcenlutions project material are to be treated as CANON for the current comprehensive build scope.

The immediate strategy is inclusion-first: expose the complete recovered vision to the building process now. Reduction, deletion, simplification, consolidation, deferral, or removal will happen later through explicit founder review rather than by silent omission during recovery or implementation planning.

## Important interpretation rules

1. This directive promotes founder-approved Transcenlutions material to CANON; it does not turn third-party research, competitor behavior, assistant suggestions that were never approved, or invented details into canon.
2. Where older source material is known to exist but its authoritative specification has not yet been recovered, preserve the item as UNRECOVERED until the source is found. Do not invent the missing specification.
3. If two founder-approved sources materially conflict, classify the conflict as CONFLICT and preserve both positions until the founder resolves it. Do not silently choose one.
4. If a later founder decision explicitly replaced an earlier decision, preserve the older item as SUPERSEDED and the replacement as CANON.
5. Preserve provenance for materially important canon so builders and future agents can trace decisions back to source material.
6. Canon describes the intended product. Build sequencing still matters: canon inclusion does not require every feature to be implemented in the same release or before its dependencies are safe and functional.
7. All future approved architectural decisions must be written back to the Master Canon Repository so no important decision exists only inside a conversation.

## Current explicit addition: Personal Throne Room & Workspace

Status: CANON

Each user may establish a persistent personal Throne Room/workspace representing whatever environment feels appropriate to that user. It is not required to be a literal throne room and may take forms such as a mansion, cabin in the woods, office, or other user-selected environment.

The environment is both personalized space and functional Transcenlutions workspace. Users may change it as often as they like for a small flat fee denominated in Crowne currency. Users may also upgrade/customize their environment over time.

Exact Crowne pricing, exchange/economic meaning, earn rates, purchase rules, and other monetary mechanics must be recovered from prior canon or explicitly decided by the founder before implementation; they must not be invented from this directive.

## Build exposure rule

Builders, coding agents, planning agents, investor materials, websites, documentation, and future Tay systems should derive product scope from the Master Canon Repository rather than whichever chat happens to be most recent.

Do not omit a canonical feature merely because it is expensive, complex, future-facing, or not part of the immediate implementation phase. Instead expose it in the system map with an appropriate dependency, phase, implementation status, or future-state marker.

The goal is ONE TRANSCENLUTIONS SOURCE OF TRUTH.
