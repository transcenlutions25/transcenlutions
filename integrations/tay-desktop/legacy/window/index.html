Talk to Tay

Choose how Tay knows you are addressing him. The microphone starts only when you press the microphone and stops after a message, when you stop it, or when the window goes into the background.

In Hey Tay mode, say “Hey Tay, …” followed by your whole request in one phrase. This is phrase matching, not recognition of your identity. Other speakers or recordings can trigger it. Messages always appear for review before sending.

Your browser may send microphone audio to its online speech service, including background speech while listening for Hey Tay. This is separate from your chat model’s local/online setting. Tay does not save raw recordings. This version does not provide offline transcription.
