How the score works

Verified = 2 points, Partial = 1, Missing / broken = 0. Not checked earns no confirmed points but remains explicitly unassessed. Each stage has 8 possible points; the overall score is earned points ÷ 40, rounded to a whole percentage. All checks have equal weight. A low score can mean missing evidence, not proven funnel failure. This is a planning aid, not a validated benchmark or a prediction of conversion or revenue.
