What you are buying

The Funnel Leak Emergency Checklist is an English-language, self-serve digital tool for business planning. It is designed for US business owners reviewing an existing offer and customer journey.

Price: USD $27, paid once. There is no subscription, recurring charge, physical shipment or shipping charge. Review the total displayed by Stripe before you pay.

You receive one standalone HTML file with 20 self-assessment checks, 20 named fixes, stage scores, assessment coverage, a prioritized action plan, progress import/export and print/save-as-PDF output.
