Privacy, simply.

Transcenlutions Arcade · version 1.0 · October 17, 2026

Your gameplay stays on your device

The game code saves your game sessions, best scores, completed levels, and display and sound preferences in this browser or app's local storage. It does not send your gameplay, scores, or settings to Transcenlutions. Clearing the app or browser's site data removes these records. There is no account or cloud synchronization.

No advertising or tracking SDKs

This version contains no ads, analytics SDKs, in-app purchases, social login, location tracking, camera access, microphone access, contact access, or user messaging. Sound, when enabled, is synthesized on your device. No recordings are made.

Web access

When you visit the web edition, your browser requests the game files from the website host. The hosting provider necessarily processes connection information, such as an IP address and request details, to deliver and secure the website. The game does not create a separate gameplay telemetry service. The installed Android package loads its bundled game files locally and requests no Internet permission.

Your controls

You can export a copy of your local progress or clear your arcade data from the Progress panel. The browser export creates a file on your device. The Android edition displays copyable progress text. Neither automatically transmits the export. Your browser and operating system may have their own backup, security, and diagnostic behavior.

Questions

Contact Transcenlutions at transcenlutions@gmail.com about this game and its privacy practices.

Changes

If a future version adds accounts, analytics, advertising, or purchases, its disclosures and relevant choices will be updated to reflect that behavior before those features are enabled.
